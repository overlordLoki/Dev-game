(press R)

Selection

Drag on the grid to select.
Ctrl+C copy · Ctrl+X cut · Ctrl+V paste · Ctrl+A all
Ctrl+drag inside the marquee moves the block · Del clears it · Esc cancels.
Tiles and objects travel together.

Doors

(press R)
Left-click a cell to put a door on it.
Right-click removes one. Clicking a door loads it into the fields above so you can re-aim it.
Side is which edge of the cell the bar sits on — face it towards the way in. With a door selected, R moves its bar; otherwise R sets the side for the next one.
Put the door on the walkable cell in front of the building — the art itself is solid, so the player can't stand on it.
Spawn is the cell they land on over there. For a way back, the destination needs its own door on that cell.
